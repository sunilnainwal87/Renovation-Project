Renovation Concept

Home Plan & Bathroom Layout

Compact 200 × 200 cm bathroom concept with blue theme, shower seating, and WC privacy partition.

Top View Layout

Plan Summary

  • Bathroom size: 200 × 200 cm
  • Door opening: 80–90 cm
  • Left return wall: 39 cm
  • WC wall (adjacent to door): 80 cm
  • Vanity on the left side
  • WC on 80 cm wall, facing shower
  • Walk-in shower under the window
  • Shower head only on right wall
  • Bench seat below shower head
  • Shower controls/taps on window wall
  • Mirror-finish glass privacy wall for WC: 70 cm (L) × 120 cm (H)
Entry view intent: the user sees the mirror glass partition first, while direct WC sightline is blocked.

Tile & Finish Choices (Blue Theme)

Feature Wall Tiles

  • Color: Deep navy blue
  • Finish: Satin/matte
  • Application: Shower zone and highlight wall

Secondary Wall Tiles

  • Color: Soft sky blue
  • Finish: Matte anti-mark
  • Application: Remaining walls for brighter look

Floor Tiles

  • Color: Blue-grey stone tone
  • Finish: Anti-slip textured
  • Application: Full floor + shower floor continuity

Fittings & Glass

  • Fixtures: Brushed nickel/chrome
  • Vanity top: Light quartz or stone
  • Glass: Frameless shower panel + mirror privacy wall

Fixture & Utility Notes

  • Keep shower controls on window wall to avoid obstruction while seated.
  • Provide adequate elbow room around vanity and WC circulation path.
  • Use moisture-resistant mirror treatment on privacy glass panel.
  • Add warm LED backlight for mirror and soft ambient lighting in shower area.
